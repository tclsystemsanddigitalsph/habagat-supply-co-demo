How we choose stock

  • It has to survive a year of ordinary use.
  • It has to be repairable or easy to clean.
  • It has to come from a supplier we can call on the phone.

Where we work

Visiting the unit

We are on the ground floor, just past the corner building. Look for the dark green sign. Parking is available along the side street in the afternoons.

Pickup orders are usually ready within a few hours during opening hours. Bring your reference number — we keep orders on the shelf by reference.

Our full address, opening hours and contact numbers are in the footer and on the contact page.
